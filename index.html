Welcome to KhannaVille

A digital time capsule from the early 2000s. Originally hosted on a dual-processor Power Macintosh G4 right here in Dartmouth, Massachusetts, KhannaVille was a personal archive of travels, adventures, and family life — rare for its time.

The memories are all still here. The site just got a fresh coat of paint.

About KhannaVille

💾

The Original

Launched around 2002, KhannaVille was a personal website served from a Power Macintosh G4 running Mac OS X and Apache HTTP Server. A family home page — a rarity in those early internet days.

🗺️

The Collections

Over the years it captured travels across America and Europe — Cape Cod beaches, German castles, Maine wilderness, New England autumn — each gallery a chapter of the story.

🎬

The Memories

Beyond photos, home videos captured graduation days, bike rides, band performances, and family visits. All of it preserved here, exactly as it was.
