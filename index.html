Welcome to KhannaVille

A digital time capsule from the early 2000s. Originally hosted on a dual-processor G4 Power Macintosh in Dartmouth, Massachusetts, this site captured cherished moments, adventures, and memories.

Today, we've preserved all that content in a modern home—same memories, fresh design.

📷

About KhannaVille

💾

The Original

Launched in the early 2000s, KhannaVille was a personal website served from a Power Macintosh running Mac OS X and Apache. It was a showcase of adventures, travels, and family moments—a rarity in those early internet days.

🗂️

The Collections

Over a decade, we captured travels across America, Europe, and New England. From Cape Cod beaches to German castles, Maine woods to autumn festivals—each gallery tells a story.

🎬

The Memories

Beyond photos, we preserved videos of cherished moments—graduation days, bike adventures, band performances, and visits with loved ones. All preserved here.
